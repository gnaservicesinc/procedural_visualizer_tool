# Music detection terminology review

Reviewed all 12 new/changed source messages in English, German, and French.
German uses Erkennungsmethode, Spektralfluss, and Noteneinsätze; French uses
méthode de détection, flux spectral, and attaques de notes. Neighbor flux is
explained as a frequency-neighborhood comparison rather than an instrument
classifier. Both translations retain the nearby-note and bass-drum tradeoffs,
the Music-only scope, named-range inheritance, and reanalysis-before-commit rule.
The default remains explicitly marked. Labels, descriptions, and the Music
processing button are translated; Qt extraction and catalog checks are required.
